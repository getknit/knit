Golden frames for MeshcoreProtoTest and MeshcoreFakeVsSimTest.

Each *.frames.jsonl line is one companion-protocol frame, app to radio ("tx") or radio to app ("rx"), as the
unmodified MeshCore companion firmware at tag companion-v1.18.0 produced it, driven frame by frame in a simulator on
2026-11-13 (~/source/meshcore-spike/FINDINGS.md, Q11-Q15; run r1). Copied unedited from that run's frames files. The
keys in them are the simulator's throwaway identities.
