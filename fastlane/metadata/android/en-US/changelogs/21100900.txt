Knit in five new languages, translation on your phone, and speech turned into text

Knit now comes in Brazilian Portuguese, Spanish, Ukrainian, French and German, and it translates messages:
long-press one and pick Translate. Translation runs on your phone, between English and those five languages,
and after a one-time download for each language it works offline. Knit can translate your own message before
you send it, too. English speech becomes text on your phone: tap Transcribe on a voice message, turn on CC in
a call, or read walkie-talkie bursts as they play.

A private internet relay can now run shared rooms, and a new switch lets Knit connect you straight to a
contact over the Internet when a relay is slow. You can make text bold or italic, pin up to three messages in
a chat, search inside one chat, and see when someone is typing over a relay. In the US, Australia and New
Zealand a LoRa board can use a frequency only Knit boards use. Calls ring with Knit's own ringtone, and stable
releases on GitHub now include a much smaller slim APK.

Fixed: a LoRa board that stopped carrying messages after Bluetooth was switched off and on, long shared text
that locked the message field, Knit staying offline after an update, "Knit isn't responding" on some phones,
the keyboard closing when you long-pressed a message, right-to-left text reading the wrong way, and Knit
closing on phones with no Files app.
