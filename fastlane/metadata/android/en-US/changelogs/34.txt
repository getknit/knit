Calls that closed Knit on mobile data, and a double Back that left a blank screen.

Knit no longer closes during a live call on mobile data. Knit told the calling library which network to
prefer by its exact mobile generation, such as 4G or 5G, but the library only accepts plain mobile data there
and shut Knit down whenever a call met one. That happened when a call started on mobile data, and mid-call
when the phone moved from Wi-Fi onto mobile data. Calls on Wi-Fi, calls to someone nearby over a direct Wi-Fi
link, and mesh calls were not affected.

Also fixed: tapping Back twice quickly now goes back one screen, where from a chat or Settings it could leave
Knit on a blank screen. Tapping a quote or a search result while someone is typing now lands on that message
instead of stopping one short. The keyboard also stays up now when you open the menu at the top of a chat.
