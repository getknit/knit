Back up to one encrypted file, pause the mesh for an hour, and use less battery

Settings now has Backup and restore. Your identity, contacts, groups and messages go into one encrypted
file, locked by a recovery key Knit shows you once, and a new phone restores it from the welcome screen.
Use it on one phone at a time; if the same backup lands on two, each one says so and offers to sign out.
The mesh notification gains Pause 15 min and Pause 1 hour beside Stop, and the mesh comes back by itself,
while Stop now keeps it off until you start it again.

Also new: tapping a file someone sent you opens it, a long chat has a button back to the newest message,
and a Meshtastic user who messages your board gets one automatic reply. A phone a parent or an organisation
manages now says who can allow a permission it turned off. A quiet mesh costs less battery, with fewer
Bluetooth sends and scans, fewer relay check-ins, and a content filter that unloads when nothing arrives.

Fixed: an empty chat list for ten seconds on older phones, a Wi-Fi Aware radio that stopped passing
messages after a busy chat, photos crossing Bluetooth three times, Wi-Fi dropping on some phones, a relay
shown as connected behind a Wi-Fi that blocked it, missing link previews, the mesh staying down after a
reboot, and searching over Wi-Fi stopping in the background on Android 10 to 12.
