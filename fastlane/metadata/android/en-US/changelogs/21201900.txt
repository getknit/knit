Videos that send on more phones, and phones no longer stuck at secure storage

On some phones, such as the Galaxy A51 and the Redmi Note 13 Pro+, every video you picked failed with
"Couldn't read that video". Those videos now send, at the same small size as on other phones.

On a phone whose security chip refuses to make keys, Knit stopped at "Waiting for secure storage" the first
time you opened it, and Try again never got past it. Knit now uses the phone's regular secure storage there
and opens as usual.

Also fixed: a crash while downloading the speech model for transcripts and call captions, gaps in the
waveform of a voice note under about four seconds long, a lock beside a chat's name that was hard to tap on
a narrow phone, and a relay invite that could name one relay while Knit connected to another.
