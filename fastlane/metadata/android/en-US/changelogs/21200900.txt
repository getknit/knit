Encrypted links between phones, Simplified Chinese, and a clearer view of who's nearby

When two phones on this version link over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi Aware, the link is now encrypted, so a radio
nearby can't read what passes between them. A link with an older version works as before until it updates,
and Diagnostics shows for each person your phone reaches whether your link to them is encrypted.

Knit now comes in Simplified Chinese. A green dot on someone's photo shows when your radios can see them, now
in the chat list and the chat header too, and the supporter badge sits after their name. Chats show a scroll
bar while you scroll, mentions get a chime of their own, and with Content filtering on, a name with slurs
shows as an alias.

Fixed: contacts you reach only over the Internet going quiet after a restore, a message request's pictures
downloading before you accepted it, a Bluetooth link that kept redialing, a lone "x" blocked as profanity,
older LoRa messages waiting another half hour after a radio came back, and a modified copy of Knit nearby
replacing a message's text.
